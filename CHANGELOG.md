# Changelog

## 0.1.0-pre — 2026-11-03

Pre-release. First dataset from the Theyyam Register.

- 102 theyyams with a Wikidata item, listed by Wikidata ID, Malayalam name and English name.
- Compiled from Malayalam Wikipedia (list of theyyams and category വർഗ്ഗം:തെയ്യങ്ങൾ) and English Wikipedia (Category:Theyyam).
- Published as a Frictionless Data Package under CC0 1.0.
